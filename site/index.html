Expert streaming does not rescue it — streaming cost scales with active parameters, and GLM-5.2 is 40B active (~13 GB of expert reads per token at ~2.6 bits/param), so several GB come off SSD every token: sub-2 tok/s optimistically. Prefill is worse, since every prompt token routes independently and touches essentially all experts. The sane use of GLM-5.2 is remote, as a planner over its native API, with local doing execution.
